# BigEastBot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

This policy describes what the BigEastBot Reddit app ("the app") does with data. The app is
operated by the moderators of r/BigEast.

## What the app collects

**No personal information.** The app does not collect, store or share any information about
Reddit users, including usernames, posts, comments, votes, IP addresses or browsing activity.

## What the app reads

- **Public sports data from ESPN** (`site.api.espn.com`): game scores, schedules, TV networks,
  rankings, conference standings and team schedules for Big East men's basketball.
- **The installed subreddit's sidebar and sidebar widgets**, so it can tell whether they need
  updating.

## What the app writes

- **The subreddit's sidebar:** the old-Reddit sidebar wiki page (`config/sidebar`) and two
  new-Reddit sidebar widgets, filled with the scores, schedule and standings above.

## What the app stores

- **One cached value:** the date of the current college basketball season's first game, kept in
  the app's Reddit-hosted storage for up to 24 hours. It contains no personal information.

## Sharing

The app sends nothing about Reddit or its users to ESPN or anyone else. Its requests to ESPN are
plain requests for public sports data.

## Reddit

The app runs on Reddit's Developer Platform. Reddit's own handling of data on its platform is
covered by the [Reddit Privacy Policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Send questions
to [r/BigEast modmail](https://www.reddit.com/message/compose?to=r/bigeast).
